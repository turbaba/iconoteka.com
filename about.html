Iconoteka is a universal, open-source library of precisely designed pictograms.

01

1 300 Icons

Iconoteka's extensive icon library supports a wide range of use cases, from rapid prototyping to real-world wayfinding systems.

7 Weights

Icon portfolio covers all extremes, featuring seven distinct weights: Thin, Ultralight, Light, Regular, Medium, Semibold, and Bold. More than enough to meet any design need.

2 Styles

Stroke and fill styles work seamlessly together, allowing instant switches from lightweight outline icons to heavy filled variants across all seven weights.

14 970 Shapes

Combining a wide range of icons multiplied by 7 weights in both fill and stroke styles, Iconoteka offers a massive library to choose from. This enables complete coverage for almost any use case, whether for personal projects, UX/UI design, or software development.

Features02

Universality

Built around geometric principles and high visual clarity, Iconoteka integrates effortlessly into varied digital environments. Its universal design language preserves legibility across products and industries that require high contrast and structural consistency.

Advanced Optics

Advanced optical compensation is applied throughout Iconoteka. Principles such as deliberate overshoots, density-based weight modularity, and optical stroke compensations give the system its refined execution and set it apart from conventional icon sets.

24-Point Grid

All icons are constructed on a 24px grid system, ensuring crisp pixel alignment for straight lines at 24px, 48px, 96px, and larger multiples. This baseline structure guarantees smooth integration alongside other popular icon libraries.

Analytics

To improve the quality of the library, Iconoteka products collect a small set of anonymous signals: activated icons, searches that return no results, icon requests, and the interface theme. This information helps clarify usage patterns, detect coverage gaps, and continuously refine the system.

Design Approach

Iconoteka is built upon years of multidisciplinary practice across graphic design, iconography, and typeface design. The result is an adaptable, highly organized icon system crafted with meticulous attention to optical balance and long-term utility.

System Architecture

Through a structured metaphor hierarchy, 5° geometric angle rule, tiered corner rounding, and modular component architecture, Iconoteka guarantees complete structural consistency. This rigor yields a scalable, adaptable library for large-scale production.
